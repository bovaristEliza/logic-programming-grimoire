# Sources & rights

Grimoire is an independent educational project about logic programming. It is
not affiliated with, endorsed by, or published by J. W. Lloyd or Springer.

## Principal reference

J. W. Lloyd, *Foundations of Logic Programming*, 2nd ed., Symbolic
Computation, Springer Berlin, Heidelberg, 1987.
[doi:10.1007/978-3-642-83189-8](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-642-83189-8).

The site's broad progression of subjects follows this reference. Its
explanations, examples, proofs, visual presentation, and original
illustrations are independently written or created for this project. Unless a
passage is clearly marked and accompanied by a specific citation, it is not
intended as a quotation from the reference.
