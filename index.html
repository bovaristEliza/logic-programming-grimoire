Grimoire

of logic programming, set down in the order of Lloyd's Foundations

Each chapter states the art precisely and then says it plainly. Definitions are written as incantations, theorems as laws, proofs are kept closed until asked for, and the margin holds the glosses. The old names are decoration; the plain name of every concept is always given beside it.
